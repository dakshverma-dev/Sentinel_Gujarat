# Camera-wall sample footage

`camera-1.mp4`, `camera-2.mp4`, and `camera-3.mp4` are eight-second excerpts (starting at 0, 8, and 16 seconds) from [`test_video.mp4` in RisAhamed/ANPR](https://github.com/RisAhamed/ANPR/blob/main/test_video.mp4). Their JPEG files are poster frames from the same excerpts. The upstream repository's MIT license is included in `UPSTREAM_LICENSE.txt`.

The video shows Delhi traffic. These clips are illustrative replays only. They are unrelated to the seeded Ahmedabad map positions and synthetic watchlist events, and they are not a Gujarat government or team-owned feed.
